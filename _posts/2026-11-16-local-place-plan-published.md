*A Place Plan for Palnackie 2025–35* is now available to read and download from the new [Documents &amp; Resources](/resources/) section of this website.

The plan is presented by Buittle Parish Community Council and was produced in association with Buittle Quest, the parish's development trust. It was funded and supported by the Dumfries &amp; Galloway Community Led Local Development Fund and Dumfries &amp; Galloway Council.

## What is a Local Place Plan?

Local Place Plans are produced by communities and set out how they would like buildings, land and development in their area to be used. Dumfries &amp; Galloway Council has encouraged communities across the region to produce them, and they feed into the Council's next Local Development Plan (LDP3), which is due in 2027.

The plan isn't a binding order. It gives the community a clear, shared statement of what matters here, which can support new projects and funding bids, and it can be updated as circumstances change. It builds on *Finding New Ways for Palnackie*, the community action plan published by the Community Council in 2018.

## Built on what you told us

In July and August 2025, 275 questionnaires were hand-delivered to households in Palnackie and across Buittle Parish, with an online version also available. These responses, together with conversations with local businesses and voluntary groups and earlier consultations, shaped the plan.

The plan looks closely at six resources that have long been at the heart of village life: the **Primary School**, the **Glenisle Inn**, the **Village Shop**, **Flounders Café**, the **Village Hall** and **the Port**.

## The plan's priorities

- **Palnackie Primary School:** find new uses for the school building that keep it serving the community, and address community asset transfer.
- **The Playpark:** repair or redesign it (and possibly relocate it in time) to make the village friendlier for young people.
- **The Glenisle Inn:** recognise and support the pub as a central part of community life.
- **The Village Shop, Flounders Café and accommodation:** support the community-owned three-part project.
- **The Village Hall:** widen the range of classes and uses, and plan improvements to the building.
- **The Port:** manage it safely as a community resource. Funding permitting, that means repairing the north wall, limiting berthing to small boats, landscaping the north shore for public use, adding a heritage cabin and building a pontoon for small boats.
- **Housing:** keep the community informed on upcoming private housing developments.
- **The Environment:** map footpaths, trails and back roads, and encourage environmental growth and awareness.

The plan also covers community action, volunteering, resilience and the role of the Community Council and Buittle Quest. It's well worth a read for anyone interested in Palnackie's future.
