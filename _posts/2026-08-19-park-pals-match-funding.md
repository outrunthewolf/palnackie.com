Great news for the Palnackie Children's Park project: Dumfries and Galloway Council have announced that they will match fund whatever the Park Pals raise.

That means the £9,000 committed by TP Niven has effectively become £18,000, and the £1,000+ already raised within Palnackie is now worth £2,000+. And that's before the group has even approached external funders, which is the next stage of work now underway.

The Park Pals group is raising funds to replace the village's ageing children's park with something safe, accessible, and welcoming for all ages — including toddlers and children with disabilities. Every pound raised locally now goes twice as far.
